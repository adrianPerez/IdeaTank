    Why the page looks this way
    • The typeface is Atkinson Hyperlegible, designed by the Braille Institute so that easily confused characters (I, l, 1 and O, 0) look clearly different.
    • Body text starts at 18px with 1.55 line spacing and lines no wider than about 70 characters, in line with WCAG 2.2 guidance on visual presentation and text spacing.
    • Text is dark gray on a warm off-white instead of pure black on pure white, which reduces glare. Every text color meets the 7:1 enhanced contrast level in both light and dark colors.
    • Tags never rely on color alone: selected tags also show a check mark and a filled shape. Buttons are at least 36 to 44 pixels tall so they are easy to hit.
    • Use Display to make text larger, add line spacing, or switch colors.
